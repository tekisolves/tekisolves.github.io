RETRO ARSENAL

Stylized 2D weapon assets for your next game.

Spend less time building every weapon from scratch and more time building your game. Retro Arsenal is a stylized retro-futurist weapon asset pack for indie developers who want a coherent visual set they can work with.

A consistent look. Multiple weapon styles. A practical starting point for your game’s visual world.

1024 × 1024 transparent PNG • SVG source • Atlas + JSON • Sold on itch.io

The problem

What this helps with

A game developer can build gameplay but may not want to spend hours creating every art asset. Retro Arsenal gives you visuals that fit together so you can keep moving on the game.

Who it’s for

Indie developers working on stylized 2D projects, including sci-fi, futuristic action, dystopian or arcade-style games where the visual language fits.

The style

Stylized retro-futurist 2D game art with clean outlines and flat, vector-inspired shapes. It has a retro sci-fi, industrial character and simplified, readable forms. It is not pixel art.

What’s included

Inside the download

  • 15 sprites: 5 weapon types across 3 tiers
  • Consistent silhouettes across the set
  • Pivots / action points
  • 1024 × 1024 transparent PNGs
  • SVG source files
  • An atlas and JSON metadata
