Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that can store or access information on your device. Some technologies are strictly necessary for a website to work, while others are optional.

2. How Teki Solves uses them

This website uses only the technologies needed to operate the site and, if you choose to allow it, Google Analytics for optional audience measurement. Teki Solves is the name under which this website and its services are currently operated.

3. Google Analytics

Google Analytics is optional. It is not loaded for analytics unless you actively select “Accept analytics” or “Allow analytics”. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics script is not loaded.

Google Analytics may use cookies such as _ga and related identifiers to distinguish users and sessions. The exact technologies used can change as Google updates the service.

4. Your choice

When you first visit, you can accept or reject optional analytics. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the “Cookie Settings” control in the website footer.

5. Third-party information

Google Analytics is provided by Google. For information about Google's processing, consult Google's own privacy documentation in addition to this policy.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy can be sent to tekisolutions2@gmail.com.
