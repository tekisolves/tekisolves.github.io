About Teki Solves

A practical mindset. A creative outlook.

Teki Solves is an independent digital product shop and creative workshop built around a simple idea: useful things should solve real problems, and making them should leave room for creativity.

We create digital products across gaming, software, assets, learning and digital art. The formats may change, but the aim stays the same: make something thoughtful that helps someone move forward.

Mission and philosophy

Solving problems simply. Empowering creativity. Making technology accessible for all.

We believe clarity is a skill, usefulness matters more than unnecessary complexity, and curiosity is worth protecting.

A good product doesn’t need to shout. It needs to do its job, communicate clearly and show care in the details.

Who it’s for

For people who make things.

Developers, self-learners, hobbyists, creators, artists and curious people arrive with different goals. What connects them is a desire to build, understand, experiment or solve something.

What we make

A growing collection of useful digital creations.

Our work can include game assets, software tools, coding products, educational material, digital assets and digital art. Our current product focus is game assets, while the wider scope remains open as the right ideas take shape.

How we think about products

Problem → product → learning.

We want each product to have a clear reason to exist. We pay attention to the problem, make something practical, explain it clearly and learn from how people use it. When a product can help someone learn as well as get something done, that’s even better.

Where we’re going

Technology, design and storytelling belong together.

Digital products are our current focus. Over time, Teki Solves aims to explore a wider creative space where technology, design and storytelling meet, including games, creative arts and hardware innovation as resources allow.
